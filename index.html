Computational Biology Research

Independent Research

  • Screened 300 CRISPRi perturbations across 491,046 human embryonic stem cells (Arc Institute Virtual Cell Challenge 2025 dataset) for gene knockdowns that lower SNCA (alpha-synuclein), a Parkinson's disease target
  • Identified TWF2 knockdown as the only perturbation meeting both prespecified criteria: 92.4% SNCA reduction while altering 14 genes, compared with 2,000+ genes for the other strong SNCA reducers (EP300, DOT1L)

  • Built the analysis pipeline in Python (scanpy, SciPy) on GPU infrastructure; code publicly available on GitHub
  • First author, "Association Between TWF2 Knockdown and Reduced SNCA Expression," American Journal of Student Research (Sept 2026); poster presented at ISMB 2026, Washington, DC
