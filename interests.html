what makes me smile

Learning & Sharing Knowledge

I like learning things and then explaining them to someone else. Taking a complicated topic apart until a friend gets it is one of my favorite feelings. That's why I write blog posts about diseases and medical history, make health resources for communities that need them, tutor classmates, and end up in late-night conversations about whatever I'm reading that week.

It's also why I keep ending up between computational biology, drug development and healthcare access. I want to understand disease at the level of molecules and pathways, and I also care about whether that knowledge reaches the people who need it. That's why I work on projects like Just a Call Away, creating multilingual health resources for immigrant communities, and why I'm interested in research that could eventually lead to treatments.
